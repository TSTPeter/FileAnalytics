Text Formatting Cleaner for Outlook

Clean text copied from Copilot, Word, or other applications. This tool removes hidden formatting and code while preserving bold, italic, underline, lists, and indentation.

How to Use:

  • Step 1: Copy formatted text from Copilot, Word, or any application
  • Step 2: Paste it into the "Input" box below
  • Step 3: Click "Clean Formatting"
  • Step 4: Copy the cleaned text from the "Output" box
  • Step 5: Paste into Outlook - no formatting issues!

What Gets Preserved:

  • Bold text (using <b> or <strong>)
  • Italic text (using <i> or <em>)
  • Underlined text (using <u>)
  • Bullet points and numbered lists
  • Basic indentation and paragraph breaks

What Gets Removed:

  • Font families, sizes, and colors
  • Background colors and highlighting
  • CSS styles and classes
  • Hidden metadata and tracking code
  • Tables, images, and complex layouts
  • Multi-dash separator lines (----------)
  • Em-dashes (replaced with regular hyphens)
  • Non-breaking spaces (replaced with regular spaces)
  • All other formatting that causes Outlook issues
